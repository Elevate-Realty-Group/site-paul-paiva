About Paul

Paul Paiva began his career in real estate in 2008 as a Mortgage Specialist, helping buyers and homeowners navigate the complexities of mortgage financing, debt consolidation, and customized lending solutions. This early experience gave him a strong foundation in understanding the financial side of real estate and a deep appreciation for the importance of putting clients first.

In 2017, Paul founded Elevate Realty Group with just two agents and a vision to build something different. Through a commitment to innovation, collaboration, exceptional client service, and a strong team culture, he has grown Elevate into one of Edmonton's leading real estate teams.

Today, Paul and his team have helped more than 2,000 families navigate their real estate journeys, building a reputation for results, relationships, and a forward-thinking approach to the industry.
